## Agent Attraction Repurposing Lens

This series is highly relevant for agent attraction because it speaks to problems agents already feel:

- Lack of a clear business plan
- Inconsistent production
- Not knowing where deals will come from
- Poor time management
- Prospecting avoidance
- Overwhelm from admin and follow-up
- Need for modern AI-enabled systems

## Potential Recruiting Angles

1. **“Most agents do not need more motivation — they need a simple operating system.”**
2. **“If your brokerage is not helping you plan the next 6 months, you are probably planning alone.”**
3. **“Your production problem may actually be a calendar problem.”**
4. **“Agents need clarity on their deal pillars before they need another lead source.”**
5. **“AI will not replace agents, but agents using AI workflows will out-execute agents who do everything manually.”**

## Output Opportunities

- Recruiting email sequence around business planning and production consistency
- Social posts targeting overwhelmed or plateaued agents
- Short-form video scripts about agent business planning mistakes
- Webinar or recruiting event: “Build Your Next 6 Months of Production”
- Brokerage positioning copy around systems, planning, accountability, and AI support
